Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Echo ("we", "us", or "our") is a music recognition app that identifies songs playing around you. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Microphone Access

Echo uses your device's microphone solely to capture short audio samples for music recognition. Audio is processed in real time and is never stored, recorded, or transmitted as raw audio. Only a fingerprint is sent to ACRCloud's recognition service.

Data We Collect

  • Your email address and display name (used for your account)
  • Song recognition history (titles, artists, timestamps)
  • Anonymous usage data to improve the app

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Raw audio recordings
  • Location data
  • Contacts or any other device data

Third-Party Services

Echo uses ACRCloud for music fingerprinting. Audio fingerprints (not raw audio) are sent to ACRCloud's servers for identification. See ACRCloud's privacy policy for details.

Data Storage

Your account data and recognition history are stored securely on our servers. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@echolistens.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, email us at privacy@echolistens.com.
